How It Works

Three steps to a fair tip

No spreadsheets. No mental math. Fair Tips handles the tax so you can focus on enjoying your meal.

Enter your bill

Type in the total shown on your receipt — including tax for now. Fair Tips will separate it out.

Detect your tax rate

The app reads your GPS location or lets you type a zip code to look up the exact local sales tax rate.

Pick your tip %

Choose 15%, 18%, 20%, or a custom amount — always applied to the pre-tax subtotal only.

See the breakdown

A clean summary shows subtotal, tax, tip, and the final total — transparent, accurate, and fair.

Features

Built for accuracy,
designed for simplicity

Feature 01

Enter any bill amount in seconds

A clean, distraction-free input makes entering your bill amount fast and error-free. Large numerals, a friendly keypad, and immediate feedback — no hunting through menus.

Feature 02

Automatic local sales tax detection

Fair Tips uses your device's location to pull the exact combined sales tax rate for your city, county, and state. No database to maintain — always current. Prefer privacy? Just type your zip code instead.

Feature 03

Crystal-clear tip summary

See every line item at a glance: pre-tax subtotal, the sales tax amount, your chosen tip, and the final total to pay. Share the breakdown with your tablemates or screenshot it for your records.
